
Data Analytics Project on Retail Price Competition
Project Title

“Price Competition Analysis of Power Drills Across Major E-Commerce Retailers”

(Alternative titles)

Retail Pricing Strategy Analysis in Digital Markets

Competitive Pricing Analysis of Amazon, Walmart, Home Depot & Lowe’s

Impact of Ads, Shipping and Inventory on Product Ranking


Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze pricing strategies and competitive behavior among major retailers (Amazon, Walmart, Home Depot, Lowe’s) selling power drills.

The project aims to understand:

How pricing differs across retailers

Whether ads influence product ranking

Impact of shipping price and delivery time on product sales rank

How inventory availability affects pricing

Identify which retailer is most competitive


Dataset Features (Important Columns)
Feature	Description
price	Product price
sale	Whether product is on promotion
shipping_price	Shipping cost
shipping_time	Delivery time
rank	Product rank in search results
ads	Sponsored ad or not
inventory	Quantity available
in_stock	Product availability
weight	Product size proxy
date	Scraping date


Key Analysis Questions
1️⃣ Price Comparison

Which retailer offers the lowest average price?

Price distribution across retailers.

2️⃣ Sales Promotion Impact

Does sale promotion reduce price significantly?

Does sale increase product ranking?

3️⃣ Ads vs Ranking

Do sponsored ads improve ranking?

Average rank of advertised vs non-advertised products.

4️⃣ Shipping Analysis

Which retailer offers fastest shipping?

Does high shipping cost reduce ranking?

5️⃣ Inventory Analysis

Does low inventory increase price?

Stock availability comparison across retailers.

6️⃣ Time Trend Analysis

Price changes from January 2020 to June 2020
